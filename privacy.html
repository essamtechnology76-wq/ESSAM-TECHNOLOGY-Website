Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ESSAM TECHNOLOGY respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use this website, why it is used and how you can contact us about it.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the form you complete, we may collect your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, business name, location, requested service, project description, budget range, preferred deadline, referral code, professional skills, experience, social profile links and other information you choose to provide.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to project enquiries and provide quotations.
  • To review partner and marketer applications.
  • To communicate about a requested service or collaboration.
  • To verify referrals and administer business operations.
  • To maintain records of enquiries and submissions.

3. Where information is processed

Website form submissions are designed to be sent to ESSAM TECHNOLOGY's Google Sheets database and may trigger email notifications through Google Apps Script and Gmail. These services process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Links to WhatsApp and GitHub open third-party services when you choose to use them.

4. Information you should not submit

Do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information through general website forms. The marketer payout request portal is not yet enabled; do not send bank account details through this website.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be accessed by people working on the relevant enquiry or project and by service providers needed to operate the website and forms. We keep records for as long as reasonably needed for communication, business administration, legal obligations and dispute resolution.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted, subject to records we may need to retain for legitimate business or legal reasons.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website may use essential browser functionality. Analytics tools are not represented as active unless and until they are configured. If analytics or additional tracking tools are added, this policy should be updated.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact essamtechnology76@gmail.com or message us through our official WhatsApp contact.

This policy is a practical website notice and is not legal advice. ESSAM TECHNOLOGY should review it against the laws and business practices that apply to its operations.
